Privacy Policy — Jigsaw Puzzles

Effective date: 18 September 2026 · Package: com.khalidgames.jigsaw

Jigsaw Puzzles ("the app") is an offline puzzle game. This policy explains what data the app handles.

1. Data we collect

We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. The app has no account system, no login, no analytics SDK, and no advertising SDK in this version.

2. Data stored on your device

To make the game work, the app stores the following locally on your device only: your coin balance, completed puzzles, daily streak, settings (language, sound, haptics, board style), and the state of any puzzle in progress. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or use "Reset progress" in Settings.

3. Permissions

The app requests no sensitive permissions. It may use the vibration function for light haptic feedback, which you can disable in Settings.

4. Internet access

The app works fully offline. All puzzle images are bundled inside the app.

5. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

6. Third-party content

Puzzle photographs are provided under the Pexels License. No third-party service receives data from the app.

7. Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example ads or purchases), this policy will be updated before that version is released.

8. Contact

Questions: mk2018ksa@gmail.com
